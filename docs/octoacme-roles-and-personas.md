# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness, deployment timing, and rollback planning so changes can move to production with clear ownership and low operational risk.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release plan, release calendar, and go/no-go checkpoints
- Confirm pre-release requirements, approvals, and deployment dependencies
- Coordinate rollback, incident, and communication plans with support and engineering
- Track release status, production verification, and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Deliver predictable, low-risk releases
- Reduce release-related delays and production incidents
- Keep stakeholders informed before, during, and after deployment

### Typical Communication
- Align with Project Managers on release milestones, risks, and dependencies
- Partner with Product Managers on release scope, timing, and customer communication
- Coordinate with Developers and QA on readiness, validation, and rollback steps
- Notify Stakeholders and Support when releases are scheduled, completed, or delayed

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria that help delivery teams make consistent decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business processes, requirements, and edge cases
- Document user flows, assumptions, and acceptance criteria
- Support backlog refinement and requirement traceability
- Help validate that delivered work matches business intent

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in planning and execution
- Improve shared understanding across business and delivery teams
- Increase the quality of requirements and acceptance outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Work with Product Managers to refine problem statements, priorities, and success measures
- Support Project Managers with scope clarification and dependency discovery
- Collaborate with Developers and QA on requirements, edge cases, and acceptance criteria
- Confirm business expectations and open questions with Stakeholders

---

## Scrum Masters / Delivery Facilitators

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Delivery Facilitators support team rhythm, remove delivery impediments, and help the team continuously improve how work moves from planning to release.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Surface blockers, team health issues, and workflow bottlenecks
- Coach the team on delivery practices, focus, and continuous improvement
- Help maintain a sustainable cadence and clear follow-through on action items

### Goals
- Improve flow, predictability, and team collaboration
- Remove friction that slows delivery
- Strengthen team accountability and learning

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Project Managers on delivery cadence, blockers, and escalations
- Align with Product Managers on backlog readiness and sprint goals
- Support Developers and QA by removing impediments and clarifying handoffs
- Share team progress, risks, and improvement actions with Stakeholders when needed

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction, architecture decisions, and implementation trade-offs so the team can deliver maintainable solutions with appropriate risk control.

### Responsibilities
- Lead solution design and technical decision-making
- Review implementation approaches, dependencies, and technical risks
- Support estimation, sequencing, and non-functional requirements
- Mentor Developers and help coordinate cross-system changes

### Goals
- Maintain solution quality, scalability, and operability
- Reduce technical rework and implementation risk
- Keep technical decisions aligned with delivery and product goals

### Typical Communication
- Advise Project Managers on technical risks, estimates, and dependency impacts
- Partner with Product Managers on scope trade-offs and feasibility
- Guide Developers on architecture, design reviews, and implementation standards
- Work with QA on quality risks, test strategy, and critical validation areas

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Representatives bring production context into planning and release decisions, helping teams prepare for operational needs, customer impact, and post-release support.

### Responsibilities
- Share support trends, operational constraints, and readiness requirements
- Contribute to incident preparation, monitoring expectations, and runbooks
- Validate support handoff needs for releases, hotfixes, and known issues
- Provide feedback from production issues and customer-facing support signals

### Goals
- Improve production readiness and service continuity
- Reduce operational surprises during release and support handoff
- Ensure customer-impacting issues are visible to delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate with Release Managers and Project Managers on release readiness and incident planning
- Inform Product Managers about customer pain points, usage issues, and support trends
- Partner with Developers and QA on observability, known issues, and reproduction details
- Keep Stakeholders informed about operational risks, support impact, and post-release follow-up
